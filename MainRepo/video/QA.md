# Q&A cheat sheet (6 minutes with AEP engineers)

Numbers below are from the integrated build (replay seed 8, Monte Carlo seed 42). Re-check after any tuning.

- **Where does "4 to 12 hours" come from?** Real labor hours per damage type from AEP's workbook (pole 4.8 h, fuse 1.2 h, ...) plus access/equipment adders, ×4.5 for travel and nonproductive time; crews from a regional pool of 150 split by expected repair hours; 1,000 Monte Carlo runs; we show P10 to P90.
- **Is the replay realistic?** Replayed repairs finish in 0/1/2/3+ days at 34/44/18/5% vs real Feb 2025 ARCOS 39/33/18/10%. Run `python -m app.models.calibrate_restoration`.
- **Is the outage map real?** Damage and network are real. The live feed is simulated: 1,185 real Feb 13-18 2025 damage reports are replayed on a clock and traced through the real upstream map (`matched_upstream.txt`) to the homes each one knocks out (about 23,000 homes go out, peak about 9,600). AEP's real outage feed would plug into the same slot.
- **Why did Maya's estimate change?** The estimate only knows damage reported so far (A13). At 3:41 AM it said 4-12 h. At 9:29 AM a second damaged spot on her path was reported, so the return time moved about 7 h later and she got an "estimate updated" message. Power came back at 6:16 PM, inside the updated range; the first range was early. We show that recap on purpose.
- **How often is the first estimate right?** Honest answer: across all homes in the replay, the real restore time lands inside the first P10-P90 range about 45% of the time; most misses are "early" because later damage hadn't been reported yet. That's exactly why we send updates. Next step: add expected not-yet-reported damage to the first estimate.
- **How good is the risk band?** Trained on Sep 2024 only, tested on Feb 2025 with history cut before the storm: AUC 0.70; the High band (top 15% of homes) held 33% of homes that later lost power (2.2× its share). Learned part alone does not beat the simple "past damage on your circuit" rule; the hybrid beats both. See `app/models/RISK_MODEL.md`.
- **Why can't some homes get a band?** 48 circuits had no damage before Feb 2025, so those homes (about a third) get "We can't predict this reliably" and a link to the official outage page instead of a guess.
- **What does "switching" do?** Network assumption. On: only repairs on your own path matter. Off: you wait for every repair on your line (Maya: about +23 h).
- **Privacy?** Salted-hash home ids, ~1 km cells with 10+ homes, no addresses or road names (map tiles capped at zoom 14), no device/circuit ids, no crew info, life-support flags never used, declared needs never stored or logged. `tests/test_d_privacy.py` scans every endpoint.
- **What if the AI makes something up?** Numbers come only from tools; every number in an LLM reply must appear in tool output or the template answer is sent instead; safety and medical text are fixed strings; emergencies get a fixed 911 reply before any model runs. Works with no LLM key at all.
- **Alerts?** Our documented rules (not AEP policy): pre-storm if Medium/High, outage confirmed, estimate moved > 3 h or 25%, restored. SMS when the phone is in low-power mode; a call offer when a medical need is declared and the outage may last over 12 h.
- **What would you need next?** AEP's live outage/AMI feed, real ETRs, weather forecasts, more regions and storms.
